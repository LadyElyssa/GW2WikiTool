GW2 Wiki Coordinate → In-Game Coordinate Converter

Converts a wiki/world-map ("continent") coordinate pair back into raw in-game coordinates, using a map's map_rect and continent_rect from the GW2 API.

1. Map rectangles

Look these up for your map via api.guildwars2.com/v2/continents/1/floors?ids=1 (continent 1 = Tyria, floor 1; use floor 2 for the End of Dragons–expanded areas). Find your map by name and copy its map_rect and continent_rect arrays.

map_rect [[minX, minY], [maxX, maxY]]
continent_rect [[minX, minY], [maxX, maxY]]

2. Wiki / world-map coordinate to convert

Wiki coordinate
Result will appear here. (Defaults pre-filled with Queensdale example — should give [9835, -17597].)
